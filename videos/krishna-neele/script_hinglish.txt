Shri Krishna neele kyun dikhte hain? Aapne Putana ke vish wali baat suni hogi. Par woh teen jawaabon mein se sirf ek hai.
Pehla jawab, lok kathaon se. Maana jaata hai ki bachpan mein Putana ne vish wala doodh pilaya, aur kuch kathaon mein Kaliya naag ke vish ki baat bhi aati hai. Kehte hain isi se unka rang neela pad gaya.
Doosra jawab, shastron mein hai. Vishnu Puran mein ek adbhut katha hai. Bhagwan Vishnu ne apne do kesh diye, ek safed, ek shyam. Safed kesh bane Balram ji, aur shyam kesh bane Shri Krishna.
Phir Shrimad Bhagavat ke mutabik, naamkaran ke samay Garg Rishi ne Nand Baba se kaha: yeh balak pichhle yugon mein safed, laal aur peele rang mein aaya tha. Is baar saanwle rang mein aaya hai, isliye iska naam hoga Krishna.
Sanskrit mein "Krishna" ka matlab hi hota hai saanwla. Isiliye unhe Ghanshyam bhi kehte hain, baarish wale baadal jaisa gehra rang. Chitrakaron ne isi gehre rang ko neele mein dikhaya.
Aur teesra jawab, sabse gehra. Aasmaan aur samudra, dono neele hain, aur dono ka koi ant nahi. Maana jaata hai ki neela rang Shri Krishna ke anant roop ka prateek hai.
