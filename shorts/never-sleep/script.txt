What if you never slept again?
Good news: 8 free hours a day.
Bad news: soon you'd see things that aren't there.
After just 24 hours awake, research says your brain works about as well as a drunk person's.
Zero drinks. Fully wasted.
Then your brain stops asking permission.
Mid-conversation, it shuts off for a few seconds.
You won't even notice. It's called a microsleep.
In 1964, a student named Randy Gardner stayed awake about 11 days.
By the end, he was seeing things and forgetting things.
And lab rats kept from sleeping? They didn't make it past a few weeks.
