# What if you had 10 arms for a day? (WhatIfyTV, Hinglish, 9:16)

## Facts
- An octopus has ~500 million neurons; about two thirds of them sit in its eight arms, which can sense and move partly on their own (Godfrey-Smith 2016; Hochner 2012, "An embodied view of octopus neurobiology", Current Biology).
- The motor cortex has a body map (the motor homunculus) laid out for one body plan: two hands. Extra limbs have no map of their own to use.
- More arms means more muscle mass to feed: muscle burns energy even at rest, more so when it moves.
